Operation formulas use 32-bit registers. C is Carry; N, Z, C, V are condition flags. In Python, u32(x) = x & 0xFFFFFFFF, register names are ordinary sample variables, and fake_memory = {0x1000: 25} is an invented dictionary.

Status flags in APSR. Q records saturation; branch conditions use N, Z, C, and V.
FlagMeaningWhen does it become 1?
NNegativeThe most significant bit of the result is 1.
ZZeroThe result is zero.
CCarryAddition carries out of bit 31; subtraction has no borrow; a shift can copy out a bit.
VOverflowThe signed result does not fit in 32 bits.
QSaturationA saturating operation has clamped a result; Q stays set until cleared.
